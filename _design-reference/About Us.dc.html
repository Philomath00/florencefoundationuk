Where Florence Foundation works

WHO WE ARE

Building Hope. Empowering Communities.

Florence Foundation is dedicated to creating meaningful change in marginalised communities by addressing poverty, hunger, healthcare needs, access to clean water, educational inequality, and barriers to women's empowerment.

We believe in working collaboratively with communities and local partners to develop sustainable solutions. Our work is guided by compassion, dignity, accountability, transparency, and a commitment to making a meaningful difference.

OUR MISSION

Fostering hope and positive change in marginalised communities.

To foster hope and positive change in marginalised communities through poverty alleviation, clean water solutions, healthcare, women's empowerment, education, and sustainable humanitarian initiatives.

Across the United Kingdom, Nigeria, and the African continent, we address critical challenges, alleviate suffering, and create opportunities for individuals and communities to thrive.

OUR VISION

Empowering individuals to thrive with dignity and hope.

To address poverty, inequality, and gender-based violence through initiatives that enable individuals to thrive with dignity, opportunity, and hope.

Our strategic initiatives in education, healthcare, and disaster relief are aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

WHAT WE DO

Addressing urgent needs. Supporting long-term wellbeing.

Our initiatives focus on poverty alleviation, food security, clean water, healthcare, women's empowerment, and education.
